Sad Rowlet

A Once Cheerful pokemon has turned depressed after seeing Pokemon Z-A having no mega evolution for it's final evolution.

Abilities

  • Leaf storm — Flip a coin. If Tails, the attack (200 Damage) succeeds
  • Roll and wheep — Roll a D20. 1-15: Miss, 16-20: Hit (100 Damage)
  • Depressed Pull — Flip a coin. If Tails, Pull a Card. If heads, lose Rowlet
